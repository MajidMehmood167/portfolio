ScholarshipHive Edutancy — Study Abroad Website

A study abroad consultancy site that's live and taking enquiries. Fourteen country pages, study visa and IELTS services, scholarship listings and partner universities, with every call to action pointed at WhatsApp because that's where their students actually reply.

02

PlotHub — Intelligent Architectural Visualization

My final year project. Give it a plot size, an orientation and a layout variation, and it draws the full 2D blueprint, then lets you rotate the same house in 3D. Works with no internet at all. Designs save as JSON, so you can export one and load it back later.

JavaAndroid StudioJSONCanvas2D/3D Rendering
03

Agri Track — Smart Crop & Farm Records

Built for a fertilizer company whose staff were keeping farmer records on paper. Enter the name, land in acres and crop once, then pull the whole record back with nothing but a phone number. Firebase keeps it synced, SQLite keeps it working when the signal drops.

JavaAndroid StudioFirebaseSQLite
